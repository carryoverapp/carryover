What it does

Every AI you use, finally talking to each other

Each AI app keeps its own history and forgets the others. Carryover connects them on your machine.

Memory graph

All your chats as one map, grouped by app, topic, project or time. Search everything and click any node to read the chat.

Carry a chat over

Hit a usage limit? Drag a chat onto another app. Carryover writes a handoff summary so you can continue in Codex, Claude Code, Claude or ChatGPT without starting over.

Compare models

Send one prompt to several models at once and watch the answers stream in side by side.

Fair judge

A model you pick scores the answers without knowing who wrote them, twice with the order swapped. Your own leaderboard shows which AI wins at what.

One memory, your rules

Short facts about how you work, learned from your own messages. You approve each one before it's shared with Claude Code, Codex or any MCP app.

Set up in a click

Finds the AI apps on your computer and shows the install or sign-in step for each, with a play button that runs it after you approve.

Private by design

Your chats never leave your computer unless you send them

Carryover has no account, no server and no tracking. It reads chat history you already have and stores it in a database on your own disk.

  • Works with free local models. Use Ollama and nothing goes online at all.
  • Tells you before anything is sent. When you pick an online model, Carryover names the company the text goes to and asks first.
  • Removes secrets first. A local filter strips emails, phone numbers, API keys, tokens and card numbers before text reaches any model.
  • Never touches your apps' history. It only reads. Forget a chat in Carryover and the original stays where it was.
  • Encrypted backups. Everything in one password-locked file you keep.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes

Download and install

Run Carryover-Windows-x64-setup.exe. It installs just for you, so no admin rights are needed.

Pick your apps

Carryover finds Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity on its own. For Claude and ChatGPT, drop in the .zip from their data export.

Explore

Open the memory graph, compare models, or carry a chat to another AI. Sign in to at least one model, or install Ollama to stay fully local.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's normal for new free apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
FAQ

Questions

Does Carryover upload my chats anywhere?

No. There's no Carryover server. Your chats are stored in a database on your computer. Text only goes online when you choose an online model for a task, such as summarizing a chat with Claude, and Carryover tells you which company receives it before anything is sent.

Do I need API keys or a paid plan?

No. Carryover uses the AI command-line tools you already have and are signed in to (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI), or free local models through Ollama.

Can it move a chat from Claude to ChatGPT?

It carries it over. Carryover writes a short handoff summary of the chat (goal, decisions, open questions, next step) and opens the other app so you can continue there. The original chat stays where it was, because Carryover never writes to or deletes from other apps.

How do Claude and ChatGPT chats get in?

Through their official data exports. In Claude or ChatGPT settings, request your data export, then drop the .zip you receive into Carryover. Nothing is scraped.

Is there a Mac version?

It's coming. The first release is for Windows 10 and 11.
